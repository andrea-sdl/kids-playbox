# The Forest Remembers

The walking landscapes, guardian cels, title, field journal map, bark drawing surface and icons use original bitmap artwork.

## Art direction

The visual references were the woodland scale of *My Neighbor Totoro*, the abandoned machinery of *Castle in the Sky*, and the quiet railway of *Spirited Away*. The guardian, spacecraft, locations, and encounters were designed for this game. [My Neighbor Totoro](https://www.ghibli.jp/works/totoro/) · [Castle in the Sky](https://www.ghibli.jp/works/laputa/) · [Spirited Away](https://www.ghibli.jp/works/chihiro/)

## Generation briefs

| Asset group | Prompt direction |
|---|---|
| Environment plates | Paint widescreen forest views at sunrise. Use gouache foliage, amber sun and cool mist, hand-inked details, and a clear foreground path. Leave the people and the robot out of the plates. Stage a flower-covered log in a meadow, a carved cedar in a grove, a gassho farmhouse and its tatami tea room, a flooded railway halt, a ruined brass observatory, and a cleft behind a waterfall. Paint open sky above the clouds for the final flight. |
| Guardian | Draw a gentle, massive forest caretaker with chipped sage enamel, a round shoulder shell, two warm eyes, heavy articulated limbs, moss and dangling roots. Draw full silhouettes facing right on transparent backgrounds. Hold the same design through rest, contact and passing steps, crouch, half-rise, extension, takeoff and flight. |
| Title shot | Use the guardian model as reference. Frame it on the right among meadow grasses. A dark beech canopy on the left gives way to a misty valley, swallows, peach sky, and golden morning light. |
| Craft and ignition | Draw a small mossy ivory aircraft with a blue cockpit, red maintenance button, worn brass and wildflowers. Draw fallen branches separately. Frame a close shot of the guardian’s iron fingertip pressing the button, with a fern, chipped enamel and low forest light. |
| Swallow and props | Draw four wingbeat cels of a slate-blue swallow with an ochre throat. Draw the coin, seeds, bell, owl, postbox, quilt, music cylinder, incense fox, paper boat, fishing float, carp and moon moth as isolated cel props. |
| Discovery inserts | Paint intimate close shots of the encounters in groups of four. Show the tea beside the hearth, a minute hand beside a silver carp, a leaf in a postbox, a glowing glass float, an owl in a hollow, a constellation on moss, and the other objects listed in the journal. Keep figures and lettering out of the frame. |
| Map | Draw the valley on warm washi paper in sepia ink and muted watercolor. Place the meadow to the west, the cedars at the center, the house to the north, the station to the south, and the observatory and waterfall to the east. |

## The walking road and discovery films

The walking camera tracks the guardian across twelve adjoining paintings. The guardian, swallow and props use drawn cels. The farmhouse interior and final flight have their own painted backgrounds. The meadow holds the only visible sun. Farther east, painted cloud bands and ridgelines carry the morning light. Each discovery card holds one background painting while transparent foreground cels move. The semaphore arm turns on its pin and the lamp lights as it seats. The moth cycles through wing drawings as it lifts from the moss and lands. The journal uses the same object cards.
